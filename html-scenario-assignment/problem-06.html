TechFest 2026

Introduction

TechFest 2026 is a fictional college technology festival designed to bring together students, innovators, developers, and technology enthusiasts. The event will provide an exciting platform to explore emerging technologies, participate in competitions, attend expert sessions, and showcase innovative projects.

Main Announcement: Registrations for TechFest 2026 are now open! Students are invited to participate and demonstrate their creativity, technical knowledge, and problem-solving skills.

Highlights

TechFest 2026 will feature a variety of exciting activities, including coding competitions, hackathons, robotics challenges, project exhibitions, gaming competitions, and technology workshops.

Participants will also have opportunities to interact with other students, work in teams, develop practical solutions, and learn about technologies such as Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Cybersecurity, Cloud Computing, and Web Development.

Date Venue

Date: October 10–11, 2026
Venue: GLA University Campus, Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh
Time: 9:00 AM onwards

The festival will take place across multiple venues on campus, including auditoriums, computer laboratories, and dedicated exhibition areas.

Speakers

TechFest 2026 will feature guest speakers and technology professionals who will share their knowledge and experiences in areas such as artificial intelligence, software development, entrepreneurship, and emerging technologies.

Interactive sessions will allow students to learn about current industry trends, career opportunities, and the skills required to succeed in the technology sector.

Registration

Students can register individually or as a team for selected competitions and activities.

Registration Deadline: September 30, 2026

Participants should complete their registration before the deadline and carefully review the eligibility criteria and event guidelines.
