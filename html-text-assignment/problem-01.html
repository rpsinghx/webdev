GLA UNIVERSITY NOIDA

About my college

University's Noida Campus is located at 15A, Knowledge Park II, Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh, making it part of one of the region's most established educational hubs. The campus holds several prestigious recognitions from major government councils such as the UGC, AICTE, BCI, and PCI, and is an active member of the Association of Indian Universities (AIU), while also carrying an "A+" grade from NAAC. As an extension of the main GLA University in Mathura, the Noida campus was set up to bring the same quality of education closer to students in the Delhi-NCR region, combining a modern, tech-enabled campus with the industry connections that come from being situated in a major commercial and IT hub.

Courses Offered

The Noida campus keeps its academic offerings deliberately focused rather than sprawling. It provides a focused selection of professional degrees including BTech, BBA, MBA, and MCA, with these courses spread across six different specialisations designed to meet industry standards. Admission to these programs is based on merit along with valid scores from accepted entrance exams — for instance, BTech and BCA applicants need at least 50% in Class 12 with PCM/CS, while MBA applicants need a minimum 60% aggregate in graduation, with exams like JEE Mains, GLAET, and CAT accepted for different courses. This tighter course structure allows the campus to concentrate resources on strengthening its management and technology programs, giving students in these streams a more specialized, industry-aligned learning experience.

Campus Life

Campus life at the Noida campus is built around a blend of comfort, safety, and everyday convenience for students. Facilities include a library, separate boys' and girls' hostels, a cafeteria, and an auditorium, with hostels offering home-like amenities such as private beds, wardrobes, and laundry and ironing services. Beyond academics, students have access to tech-enabled classrooms with projectors, specialised department libraries, computer labs, and 24/7 medical support through the university's Aarogyam service, along with sports facilities like basketball, football, and various indoor games. The relatively compact size of the campus compared to the main Mathura campus tends to foster a closer-knit student community, where clubs, cultural events, and peer interactions feel more personal and easier to be part of.

Why I Like My College

What stands out most about the Noida campus is how closely it ties academics to real industry exposure, largely thanks to its location in the Delhi-NCR corridor. Students move from classroom discussion into action — building prototypes, working on live problems, and getting feedback from industry mentors rather than just sitting through lectures. The proximity to a major business and tech ecosystem means internship and placement opportunities feel within easy reach, and the smaller, more focused set of programs means faculty and students often know each other well, creating a more personal learning environment. Combined with modern facilities and a genuine push toward practical, project-based learning, it's a campus that manages to feel both ambitious and approachable at the same time.
