Student Terms & Rules


General Rules

Students must carry their college ID card while attending classes, laboratories, examinations, or other official activities.

Students must maintain cleanliness in classrooms, laboratories, corridors, and other college areas.

Students are expected to respect college property and use all facilities responsibly.


Attendance

Students must maintain the required attendance percentage throughout the academic semester.

Students must attend classes regularly and arrive on time.

Students falling below the required attendance may be restricted from appearing in examinations according to institutional rules.

Medical or other genuine reasons for absence should be reported to the appropriate authority with valid documentation.


Examination

Students must carry their valid ID card and examination admit card when appearing for an examination.

Students must not use unauthorized materials or electronic devices during examinations.

Any form of unfair means may result in disciplinary action or cancellation of the examination.

Students should reach the examination hall before the reporting time to avoid unnecessary problems.


Assignment Submission

Students must submit assignments before the given deadline.

Late submissions may receive reduced marks or may not be accepted after the final submission deadline.

Assignments must contain the student's name, roll number, course, and submission date.

Students should keep a copy of their submitted work for future reference.


Discipline

Students must behave respectfully towards teachers, staff members, and fellow students.

Students must not engage in bullying, harassment, vandalism, or disruptive behaviour on campus.

Maintaining discipline helps create a safe, respectful, and positive learning environment for everyone.


Important Notice

All students are responsible for reading and following the rules of the institution. Failure to follow these rules may lead to appropriate action according to college policies.

Important: Assignment deadlines, examination schedules, and other academic notices must be checked regularly.
